# AP Lang: Advertisement Analysis - Audience & Purpose

**Date**: September 4, 2025  
**Subject/Topic**: Analyzing How Advertisements Appeal to Audiences to Achieve Purpose  
**Duration**: 50 minutes (full class period)  
**Class/Period**: AP Language & Composition  

## Lesson Overview

**Learning Target**: 
- I can analyze how advertisements appeal to audiences to achieve a purpose.

**Objectives**: 
- [ ] Define and differentiate demographics vs. psychographics in target audiences
- [ ] Apply a systematic process for analyzing advertisement audience and purpose
- [ ] Identify specific strategies advertisers use to appeal to audience vulnerabilities, values, and emotions
- [ ] Evaluate the effectiveness of advertising strategies in achieving their intended purpose

**Activities Used**:
- Interactive audience analysis cards (demographics vs. psychographics)
- Guided practice with Toyota, HelloFresh, and Dove advertisements
- Independent advertisement analysis using structured process
- Small group discussions and sharing

**Technology**: 
- Projector for advertisement examples
- Edulastic for assignment submission
- Interactive task cards for audience analysis practice

## Lesson Structure (50 minutes)

### Opening (10 minutes)
- Quick review of summer work observations
- Introduce learning target and purpose
- Preview today's agenda and homework expectations

### Direct Instruction (15 minutes)
- **Demographics vs. Psychographics** lesson using interactive cards
  - Demographics: Age, race, gender, income, education, location, occupation, etc.
  - Psychographics: Values, attitudes, interests, behaviors, fears, personality traits
  - Emphasis on In-Group vs. Out-Group dynamics

### Guided Practice (20 minutes)
**Step-by-Step Analysis Process:**

**STEP 1: Define Audience**
- Define which demographics and psychographics likely apply to their audience
- Include as many as reasonably possible based on ad's implications

**STEP 2: Analyze Strategy & Purpose**
- What about the audience are they appealing to? (emotion, values, vulnerability)
- What perception are they trying to create?
- What behavior are they trying to provoke?
- What purpose will that ultimately achieve?

**Practice Advertisements:**
1. **Toyota Camry** - Market segmentation discussion
2. **HelloFresh** - "Baths vs. showers" implications activity
3. **Dove** - Audience appeal strategies

### Independent Practice (5 minutes)
- Begin independent advertisement analysis
- Students select their own advertisement to analyze using the process
- Work time to get started on homework assignment

## Assessment
**Formative**: 
- Interactive card responses during demographics/psychographics activity
- Group discussion participation during guided practice
- Exit ticket: One thing they learned about audience analysis

**Summative**: 
- Independent Advertisement Analysis (due by 7am next class day via Edulastic)

## Materials Needed
- Advertisement Analysis Interactive Cards
- Example advertisements (Toyota, HelloFresh, Dove)
- Edulastic assignment set up
- Advertisement Analysis Process handout

## Homework Assignment
**Advertisement Analysis** - Submit on Edulastic by 7am on our next class day
- Find one advertisement (print, digital, or video)
- Complete full audience and purpose analysis using today's process
- Include both demographics and psychographics
- Analyze strategies, intended perceptions, behaviors, and ultimate purpose

## Notes for Success
- Emphasize the systematic process over "right" answers
- Encourage students to dig deep into implied audiences, not just obvious ones
- Connect to AP Lang skills: rhetorical analysis, audience awareness, purpose identification
- Use real, current advertisements for relevance and engagement

## Connection to AP Lang Skills
- **Rhetorical Triangle**: Focus on audience analysis
- **Rhetorical Appeals**: How pathos, ethos, logos target specific audiences  
- **Rhetorical Strategies**: Purpose and effect of advertising techniques
- **Analysis Writing**: Structured approach to evidence and explanation

## Differentiation
- **Visual learners**: Advertisement examples and interactive cards
- **Analytical thinkers**: Step-by-step systematic process
- **Social learners**: Group discussions and sharing
- **Independent workers**: Choice in advertisement selection for homework

## Extensions/Enrichment
- Compare how the same product is advertised to different demographics
- Analyze advertisement series or campaigns for consistency
- Research advertising psychology and manipulation techniques
- Create their own advertisement targeting a specific audience

## Vocabulary
- **Demographics**: Statistical characteristics of populations
- **Psychographics**: Psychological characteristics and behaviors  
- **Target audience**: Specific group intended to receive advertising message
- **Market segmentation**: Dividing consumers into distinct groups
- **In-group/Out-group**: Social psychology concept of belonging vs. exclusion
- **Rhetorical purpose**: The goal or intention behind persuasive communication
