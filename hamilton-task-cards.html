Hamilton: Opening Songs Task Cards

Character Analysis, Themes, and Historical Connections

EASY LEVEL (70%)
12-15 min
Hamilton's Early Life
Which events were most influential in Hamilton's early life? Find two quotes to support your answer.

Directions:

  1. Listen to "Alexander Hamilton" with your group
  2. Identify the most important events from Hamilton's childhood and youth
  3. Choose the TWO events that shaped him the most
  4. Find two specific quotes that show how these events affected him
Sentence Frame:
"The most influential events in Hamilton's early life were _______ and _______. This is shown in the quotes '...' and '...' because _______."

Helpful Hints:

  • Listen for events like being orphaned, poverty, hurricanes
  • Look for quotes that show how events changed Hamilton
  • Think about which events made Hamilton who he became
