Analogies Fill-in-the-Blank

Easy Level Answers
Card 1 Answers
cat : kitten :: dog : puppy
Relationship: Adult to Baby/Young
A cat's baby is called a kitten, just as a dog's baby is called a puppy. This shows the relationship between an adult animal and its offspring.
big : small :: hot : cold
Relationship: Antonyms/Opposites
Big and small are opposites, just as hot and cold are opposites. This shows contrasting or opposite meanings.
Card 2 Answers
hand : glove :: foot : shoe
Relationship: Object to Covering
A glove covers and protects a hand, just as a shoe covers and protects a foot. This shows what item covers or protects each body part.
book : read :: song : listen
Relationship: Object to Action
You read a book to experience it, just as you listen to a song to experience it. This shows the action you perform with each object.
Card 3 Answers
sun : day :: moon : night
Relationship: Object to Time/Context
The sun is associated with and provides light during the day, just as the moon is associated with and provides light during the night.
bird : fly :: fish : swim
Relationship: Animal to Movement
Flying is the natural way birds move through their environment, just as swimming is the natural way fish move through their environment.
Card 4 Answers
happy : sad :: up : down
Relationship: Antonyms/Opposites
Happy and sad are opposite emotions, just as up and down are opposite directions. This shows contrasting concepts.
teacher : school :: doctor : hospital
Relationship: Worker to Workplace
A teacher works at a school, just as a doctor works at a hospital. This shows the relationship between a profession and its typical workplace.
Medium Level Answers
Card 5 Answers
painter : brush :: writer : pen
Relationship: Professional to Tool
A painter uses a brush as their primary tool for creating art, just as a writer uses a pen as their primary tool for creating written works.
library : books :: museum : artifacts
Relationship: Place to Contents
A library is a place that houses and displays books, just as a museum is a place that houses and displays artifacts or artworks.
Card 6 Answers
captain : ship :: pilot : airplane
Relationship: Leader to Vehicle
A captain is the person in charge of operating and commanding a ship, just as a pilot is the person in charge of operating and commanding an airplane.
foundation : building :: roots : tree
Relationship: Support to Structure
A foundation provides structural support and stability for a building, just as roots provide support and nourishment for a tree.
Card 7 Answers
orchestra : conductor :: team : coach
Relationship: Group to Leader
A conductor leads and directs an orchestra to coordinate their performance, just as a coach leads and directs a team to coordinate their efforts.
telescope : distant :: microscope : tiny
Relationship: Tool to Purpose
A telescope is used to see distant objects clearly, just as a microscope is used to see tiny objects clearly. Both tools help us see what we normally cannot.
Card 8 Answers
architect : blueprint :: chef : recipe
Relationship: Professional to Plan
An architect creates and follows blueprints to design buildings, just as a chef creates and follows recipes to prepare dishes.
chapter : book :: scene : play
Relationship: Part to Whole
A chapter is a section or part of a book, just as a scene is a section or part of a play. Both represent subdivisions of a larger work.
Hard Level Answers
Card 9 Answers
catalyst : reaction :: inspiration : creativity
Relationship: Trigger to Result
A catalyst triggers or accelerates a chemical reaction, just as inspiration triggers or accelerates creativity. Both serve as initiating forces for a process.
eloquent : speech :: graceful : movement
Relationship: Quality to Expression
Eloquent describes the quality of refined, articulate speech, just as graceful describes the quality of refined, fluid movement.
Card 10 Answers
meticulous : precision :: spontaneous : improvisation
Relationship: Characteristic to Behavior
Being meticulous leads to precision and careful attention to detail, just as being spontaneous leads to improvisation and unplanned action.
symbiosis : mutual :: parasitism : one-sided
Relationship: Biological Relationship to Benefit Pattern
Symbiosis is a relationship where both organisms benefit mutually, while parasitism is a relationship where the benefit is one-sided (only the parasite benefits).
Card 11 Answers
ephemeral : duration :: ubiquitous : presence
Relationship: Quality to Dimension
Ephemeral describes something brief in duration (time), just as ubiquitous describes something widespread in presence (space).
cacophony : harmonious :: chaos : organized
Relationship: Negative State to Positive Opposite
Cacophony (harsh, discordant sounds) is the opposite of harmonious, just as chaos (disorder) is the opposite of organized.
Card 12 Answers
intrepid : courage :: astute : wisdom
Relationship: Trait to Underlying Quality
Being intrepid (fearlessly bold) demonstrates courage, just as being astute (perceptive and insightful) demonstrates wisdom.
verbose : concise :: opaque : transparent
Relationship: Negative Quality to Positive Opposite
Verbose (using too many words) is the opposite of concise (brief and clear), just as opaque (unclear or hard to understand) is the opposite of transparent (clear and easy to understand).
